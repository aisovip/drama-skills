# Blind reviewer brief

The fresh independent reviewer received only `fixture.md`, `arm-A.md`, and
`arm-B.md`; the mapping stayed hidden until after `blind-verdict.md` was written.
The reviewer was asked to compare:

- story and authority fidelity;
- production-form specificity rather than style-token decoration;
- cross-stage owner boundaries;
- executability for ink dynamic comic and stylized 3D;
- continuity, safety, and text handling;
- reviewer evidence versus taste;
- creative variance and over-template risk.

The reviewer had to identify concrete defects in each arm, choose A, B, or tie
with confidence, and recommend promotion, narrow-and-retest, or hold. Repository,
skills, source research, mapping, browsing, and media access were excluded.
